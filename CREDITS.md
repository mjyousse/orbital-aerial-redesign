## Sample terrain

Elevation from USGS 3DEP via [AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), and imagery from the [USGS National Map](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/national-map) orthoimagery. Both are public domain US government data. Area: west Austin, TX. Height in the 3D viewer is exaggerated 3×. The before/after pair is the same 4.8 km tile, processed into a shaded surface with 5 m contours.
